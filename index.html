Our story

Heritage, distilled

Born from the scent of bakhoor drifting through our grandparents’ majlis.

Assaf blends the ancient Arabian art of attar with modern French perfumery. We source oud from Cambodia and Assam, rose from the highlands of Taif and saffron from Khorasan — then age each composition for weeks before it is bottled.
