The house

A name that carries a memory

Assaf began with a single bottle of oud oil, blended at home for family celebrations.

Friends asked for it. Then their friends. Today our perfumers combine the Arabian tradition of attar with the precision of French perfumery — sourcing rare raw materials directly from growers, ageing every batch, and finishing every bottle by hand.

We believe a fragrance should be felt before it is noticed, and remembered long after it is gone.
