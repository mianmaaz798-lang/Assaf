Help

Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?

Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam: 1–2 business days. Rest of Saudi Arabia: 2–4 days. GCC: 3–6 days. Delivery is free on orders over SAR 400.

Can I return a fragrance?

Unopened bottles in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. Contact our concierge to arrange a pickup.

Are your perfumes long-lasting?

All Assaf fragrances are eau de parfum at 20% concentration. Most last 8–12 hours on skin and longer on clothing.

Do you offer samples?

Yes — every order includes a complimentary sample, and discovery sets are available in our boutique.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and cash on delivery within Saudi Arabia.
